Why Vividly?

If agents can build anything, why use Vividly? For one production, perhaps you don’t. Vividly gives the work shared structure. The work doesn’t end as an artifact. It becomes part of the repertoire.

Every production expands what you can direct next.

One artifact

One carving. One fixed composition.

A fixed whole

Made for this production.

A shared medium

Reusable parts. New combinations.

The repertoire

Ready to reuse, remix, and recombine.
