## Put the explanation first

Open your slide's direct URL in a browser tab, enlarge the window or go full screen, and try explaining the idea aloud:

> "Start with the same $1,000. Change just the annual rate. Over five years, what happens to the gap?"

Move the slider while you explain. You are presenting a working model, not a picture of one.

## Only the navigation you need

- **?** opens keyboard help.
- **Escape** opens the presentation overview.
- **Right / Left** move through ordinary steps and slides when focus is not inside a control.
- When the slider has focus, arrow keys adjust it. Tab away to return to presentation navigation.

We asked for a single-step slide, so there are no hidden reveals to learn.

> **You should now have:** a slide you can explain and operate comfortably. Before sharing it, test the lowest and highest rate once more.
