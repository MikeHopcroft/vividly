Labs / Drawing in parallel

One owl, multiple pens.

I wanted to use an anamorphic wire sculpture to illustrate how motion can reveal new structures and concepts. The first iteration (shown on the left) took too long to draw, yet seemed rushed and complicated. It felt as though the rapid pen movements were distracting from the reveal at the end.

I was curious whether using multiple pens would allow me to create the perception of a slower pace while also reducing the total animation time.

1 pen

3 pens

Changes rerun both drawings automatically. Each pen draws an equal share of the wire. Drawing defaults to the original 1.5 seconds, followed by its 0.15-second pause and 1.2-second rotation.
